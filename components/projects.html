PLANNED PROJECTS (2027-2029)

Both projects are being implemented within the framework of innovative solutions support programs and are planned to be launched in 2027. We invite investors and partners to work together!

1. Solar panel reception and recycling line
Development of technology for dismantling, sorting and recovery of semiconductor silicon from waste modules
  • Pilot hydromechanical separation plant
  • Scaling and integration with recycling operators
  • Analysis of solar cell composition and their degradation

2. Recycling of Vanadium Redox Batteries (VRFB)
Project for extraction and regeneration of vanadium electrolytes from spent energy storage systems
  • Chemical and membrane purification of electrolyte
  • Obtaining vanadium oxide and vanadium sulfate products
  • Development of safe methods for disposal of housings and seals
